Shipping Policy

Shipping and delivery information for Smart Kids Kuwait

1. Shipping Coverage

  • We ship within Kuwait only.
  • Free shipping on all orders above 20.000 KWD.

2. Processing Time

  • Orders are processed within 1-2 business days.
  • Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next business day.

3. Delivery Time

  • Standard delivery: 4-7 business days.
  • Delivery times may vary during peak seasons or holidays.

4. Delivery Method

  • Cash on delivery (COD) available.
  • Our delivery partner will contact you to arrange delivery time.
  • Please ensure someone is available to receive the order.

5. Delivery Address

  • Please provide accurate and complete delivery address.
  • We are not responsible for delays due to incorrect addresses.
  • Address changes after order confirmation may cause delays.

6. Order Tracking

  • You will receive order confirmation via email.
  • Tracking information will be provided when available.

7. Failed Delivery

  • If delivery fails due to customer unavailability, additional delivery charges may apply.
  • Please do not refuse delivery as it may cause processing delays.

8. Damaged Items

  • Please inspect your order upon delivery.
  • Report any damage within 24 hours of delivery.
  • We will arrange replacement for damaged items at no extra cost.

9. Shipping Restrictions

  • Some remote areas may have extended delivery times.
  • We reserve the right to use alternative shipping methods when necessary.

Contact for Shipping Inquiries

For shipping inquiries, please contact us at: kuwait-info@smart-kids.me
