Privacy Policy

How Smart Kids Kuwait handles your personal information

We are committed to respecting your privacy and recognizing your need for appropriate protection of the personally identifiable information you share with us.

You can visit our website without telling us who you are or revealing any personal information about yourself. However, in some cases, such as when making a purchase, we ask you to provide certain information, such as your name, address, phone number, email address, and other information required to complete your order.

Information We Collect

The Smart Kids Kuwait website collects certain necessary information and data and may share it with third-party services such as Google and Meta to provide better advertising services to users.

User Account Data

This includes your email address, data provided by third parties such as Google and Meta, or data you previously provided via the “Sign In” feature on our website.

User Personal Information

This includes your username and gender.

Non-Personal Contact Information

This is all data the website collects about a user's connection, the duration of their use of the website, the windows the user accesses, and the times they exit.

How We Use This Information

This information is used to improve the website and the advertising services provided to users.

Sharing Your Information

We will never share information, including your personally identifiable information, with any third party for the purpose of contacting you regarding third party products and services.
